2. RARITY LOGIC
Use exact rational/integer weights, not floating-point equality approximations. The existing implementation uses a 10-billion denominator, which CANNOT represent an exact 1/20-billion single-roll chance. Increase the integer denominator to a common multiple of ALL listed odds denominators, computed and checked programmatically using BigInt. Avoid exceeding Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER in sum, products or bulk sampler calculations. If required, use an accurate BigInt-supported discrete sampler or mathematically equivalent exact method. Preserve correctness and efficiency for up to 100 million bulk rolls. Do not approximate rare-aura probabilities into zero, round expected counts, impose pity, or fake variance.

The common Rusty Aura fills the remaining probability mass after all other entries. All auras are mutually exclusive outcomes on each roll. The sum of probabilities must be exactly one. Import/export probability displays must remain correct.

3. BALANCING GOALS
The major problem to solve is the enormous steps in odds, NOT lack of overpowered buffs. All existing aura bonuses stay the same. The strongest NEW Copper Crown is only marginally better than Copper God (+315% vs +300% copper, +155% vs +150% XP, +55% vs +50% factory speed). Keep the 1/20B drop genuinely aspirational; large batch rolls must not make it trivially obtainable. Do not buff other systems or silently increase general aura power.

Not every rarer aura must be universally stronger: some are specialized for manufacturing or XP, so make that specialization explicit. In descriptions and UI, avoid calling a niche aura a straight upgrade when its strengths are in a different category.
