# Ascension, Gold, and the 35-device expansion

This extends the existing game to 87 electronics and 87 compulsory signatures, without replacing the original 52. Chapters end at R18. All new devices require Ascension 1, their chapter, and their current-run level.

## Stage Compression

Buy seven permanent ranks, in order, with Standard copper. Costs receive no discounts. Each rank removes one supporting stage; the Advanced Workbench may remove one more eligible supporting stage. The current chapter has a floor of three stages when its original count is at least three; earlier chapters have a floor of one. Floors never increase the authored count. The final signature always remains. Bosses and both kinds of trial ignore compression.

| Rank | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | 100 g |
| 2 | 10 kg |
| 3 | 1 t |
| 4 | 100 t |
| 5 | 10 kt |
| 6 | 1 Mt |
| 7 | 100 Mt |

Each cost is exactly 100 times the preceding rank. A stale or repeated purchase cannot charge twice for the same rank. Compression survives both resets.

A1 opens at R12 or above, Level 28, after a successful #52 Experimental Energy Converter recovery in the current run. Complete the 12-stage trial, then review a separate reset confirmation. Failure spends no equipment or resources. Older R13+ saves keep their current chapter and progress until the player explicitly confirms Ascension.

### Reset confirmation

Ascension resets current Rebirth to R0, level and XP, ordinary money and all copper qualities, electronics inventory, ordinary tools and legacy tool bonuses, ordinary Prestige Points/ranks, crafted materials, temporary research, active contracts, current-run statistics, processing jobs, unsold ingots, furnace upgrades, automation, and refining facilities. This facility reset is explicitly warned about before confirmation.

It preserves the account, achievements, lifetime statistics, historical records, cosmetics, auras, Stage Compression, Ascension levels, Copper Essence, purchased Ascension skills, all gold, claims, gold equipment and Golden Engineering. Ordinary Rebirth never resets any gold progression.

### Copper Essence skills

CE skills have a separate, explicit refund confirmation that returns the exact costs of all purchased ranks. This lets players change their build and always reach Master Recycling even after spending their first 25 CE elsewhere. Automatic Ascension bonuses and completed gold research are preserved.

CE is a permanent prestige currency. It cannot be sold or traded. Each next rank costs its initial cost multiplied by the rank being bought.

The permanent base multiplier is applied automatically before purchased permanent extraction bonuses. XP bonuses apply to earned rewards; Conductive Research applies specifically to electronics. Smelting throughput shortens actual processing duration. Refining throughput increases batches per action, carrying fractional throughput forward; each completed refined batch still consumes exactly ten standard ingots. No bonus creates free ingots.

## Gold Operation

Gold unlocks at A1 and has no separate Rebirth or Ascension. All balances and lifetime panning recovery are integer milligrams. Choose an unlocked fictional claim and complete three increasingly difficult panning rounds. Tilt with the pointer, slider, keyboard arrows, or onscreen controls; shake with Space or its button to clear sediment. Keep the marker within the target pocket while managing instability. A failure grants zero reward and can be retried free.

Grades multiply the base payout: C ×1; B ×1.05; A ×1.10; S ×1.20. Permanent gold recovery bonuses apply afterward, with the final reward rounded down to whole milligrams. Every round requires interaction. Equipment cannot complete the pan for you. Only validated panning rewards increase lifetime recovered gold. Purchased gold increases spendable gold only.

## Worldwide metal markets

Copper: $0.30/g minimum, $0.60/g equilibrium, $2.00/g maximum. Typical per-tick movement is bounded at ±2% in quiet periods, ±5% active, and ±8% turbulent. Rare shocks can reach ±10%. Momentum, reversals and gradual mean recovery are server-generated.

Gold: $30/g minimum, $60/g initial/equilibrium, $180/g maximum. It uses its own worldwide quote and independent momentum, activity periods and rare shocks. Both markets tick every 10 seconds, including when nobody is playing. Trades are checked atomically at the current server quote. Purchases carry a 1% fee. Buying gold never increases recovered-gold milestones.

Each market permanently keeps its current quote and stores only the past hour of chart points, at most 361. Quote polling uses a compact combined response; only the open chart requests incremental history. Hidden tabs stop polling. There are no Realtime subscriptions. Player influence contributes at most 0.03 percentage points per player per tick and 0.15 percentage points in aggregate; server variation dominates. Six trades per player per asset per tick is the limit. Accounts, saves and necessary transaction receipts are retained.

## All 35 signature controls

### #53 — Quantum Phase Alignment

Adjust a single phase ring by ±15°. Align all four inside their green ±12° arcs, then lock and keep alignment for 2 seconds. Three bad locks fail.

### #54 — Fusion Sequence Logic

Match the five-symbol target in at most eight operations: rotate left/right, reverse the first three, or swap an adjacent pair. The last pair wraps around.

### #55 — Ultra-Precision Turbine

Select one dial. Accelerate or brake its momentum, then lock inside its ±10° target below 5°/s. Freeze all three dials. Three incorrect locks fail.

### #56 — Mega Power Distribution Array

Allocate exactly 12 units to the six destinations. Each link holds at most 5. Required demands are 2, 3, 1, 2, 4, and 0. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #57 — Titan Electromagnetic Rotor

Match the target arrows. A click turns that rotor and its linked horizontal neighbor clockwise. Every scramble has a reversible solution.

### #58 — Advanced Recovery Supercomputer

Remember four flashed symbol identities while the whole matrix rotates after each flash. Repeat the identities, then complete four rounds. Two errors fail.

### #59 — Hypergrid Power Converter

Allocate exactly 20 input units across four converters. Observe their minimums, maximums and gains; match every required output. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #60 — Planetary-Scale Generator Module

Hit each pulsing ring as it crosses the top target. Complete three rounds of six hits. Each ring has its own period; four missed beats fail.

### #61 — Industrial Magnetic Accelerator

Set each junction toward its numbered next checkpoint before the moving token arrives. Branch labels show the destination. A dead end or wrong checkpoint fails immediately.

### #62 — Mega Transformer Network

Connect all eight nodes within the displayed total link cost. Cycles are allowed if affordable. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #63 — Hyper-Density Motor Cluster

Turn every arrow upward. A rotor click turns itself clockwise and all orthogonal neighbors counterclockwise. Stay within the move budget.

### #64 — Colossal Electrical Core

Rebuild the numbered 5×5 geometric image: select two tiles to swap them and rotate each tile until its arrow faces up. Use the move budget.

### #65 — Experimental Energy Matrix

Adjust four linked integer controls. Each changes several readings. Hold all four target readings for 3 seconds. Staying outside the safe region for 10 seconds fails.

### #66 — Ultra-Industrial Logic Engine

Program the robot to collect both tokens and reach EXIT. Use Forward, Left, Right, Collect, and Repeat previous. At most 12 commands; three unsuccessful runs fail.

### #67 — Hyperconductive Power Core

Select a continuous start-to-end route that visits both marked checkpoints, keeping its total weighted cost within budget. Remove links freely. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #68 — Autonomous Recycling Megaplant

Select a job and a machine/time slot, or drag a job to a slot. Respect its machine eligibility, duration and dependencies. Schedule all six within 12 ticks.

### #69 — Electromagnetic Titan Engine

Hit 24 notes across four lanes, including paired notes and holds. Hold sustained notes until their tail clears. At least 80% accuracy; four complete misses fail. Keys 1–4.

### #70 — Quantum Recycling Array

Use the current visible shape, color, or symbol rule to sort 18 tokens correctly. Rules change after six correct tokens; three errors fail.

### #71 — Colossal Generator Matrix

Connect the source to all six marked nodes in the 5×5 grid. Respect every node’s link limit and the 14-link budget. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #72 — Hyper-Scale Turbine Engine

Balance three coupled turbine outputs inside their target bands with low momentum for four seconds. Accelerate one output and its neighbor, or brake. Avoid broad safety limits.

### #73 — Advanced Electromagnetic Grid

Solve the six visible symbol equations and enter the six-digit code. Each equation constrains the same symbols. Three incorrect submissions fail.

### #74 — Titan Industrial Reactor Module

Choose transitions in this abstract state machine. Reach the starred target within the resource budget. Entering the red forbidden state fails immediately.

### #75 — Quantum Motor Megastructure

Set a direction or wait for each robot, then advance one simultaneous turn. Reach all three goals within 12 turns. Collisions, edge swaps and blocked cells fail.

### #76 — Imperial Recycling Core

Allocate four resource types across four projects. Meet each project’s full typed requirements without exceeding any shared pool. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #77 — Ultra-Dense Generator Array

Hit 30 notes across five lanes. Later windows narrow. Paired notes and sustained holds require multiple controls. Five misses fail. Keys 1–5.

### #78 — Hyperconductive Industrial Nexus

Rotate directional tiles in both layers. ↥ is a portal and works only at a marked portal pair. Route three colored inputs to their matching outputs.

### #79 — Experimental Titan Stator

Memorize two 3×3 layers, including symbol orientations, during the eight-second preview. Rebuild both layers by swaps and rotations within the move budget.

### #80 — Quantum Energy Distribution Hub

Set integer flow on each source-to-destination link. Balance both source supplies, meet all five demands, respect capacities and stay within weighted transport cost.

### #81 — Advanced Recycling Command Core

Program collection and matching-station deposits for three materials. Loop repeats the preceding Move/Collect/Deposit block twice; Conditional Skip skips the next command if empty. Limit 14 commands.

### #82 — Hyper-Industrial Megasystem

Edit all three linked panels: A routes energy, B allocates that actual capacity, and C encodes B’s output. Final validation checks all panels together.

### #83 — Titan Quantum Power Engine

Align six coupled phase rings. Each ±30° action turns the selected ring and its two marked neighbors. Match every target within the move budget.

### #84 — Colossal Electromagnetic Nexus

Place AND, OR, XOR or NOT gates, connect each slot to a compatible source port, and match all four outputs for every visible input case. Three invalid submissions fail.

### #85 — Experimental Recycling Singularity

Reconstruct the 6×6 target using clockwise 2×2 rotations and whole-row swaps. Then merge exactly three pairs of matching symbols in matching target pockets. Stay within the move budget.

### #86 — Ultimate Industrial Power Matrix

Complete three linked phases: allocate resources, build a constrained weighted route, then synchronize three output pulses. Earlier choices determine later capacity and timing.

### #87 — Copper Empire Ascendant Core

Complete four Ascendant sections: remember six symbols, allocate ten resource units, lock three moving rings, then connect the final network. Any section failure restarts the entire signature.

## Validation and deployment

The expansion is tested in milestones: additive save defaults, exact catalogue checks against the owner's specification, all new game control recordings, real server replay, failure and duplicate reward protection, the A1–A5 reset rules, panning rewards, both markets' SQL transactions, browser isolation and hour retention. Seeded puzzle witnesses use ordinary actions, not a win or skip command. The original 52-game tests remain.
