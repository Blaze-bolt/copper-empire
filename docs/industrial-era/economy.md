Full staff on a single F01 job is also unprofitable at equilibrium. Workers should be assigned selectively or costs amortized over funded batches. This is a market-sensitive manufacturing economy, not guaranteed profit.

Smallest proposed correction: **keep every authored reference price and recipe**, show accurate net margin and a loss confirmation before funding, and allow players to wait for favorable copper/export quotes. Do not require a loss-making factory job for progression. Freight basis and loss confirmation behavior need owner decision before the shipping economy is enabled. Raising every reference price to guarantee profit at the copper maximum would distort the authored economy and is not recommended.
